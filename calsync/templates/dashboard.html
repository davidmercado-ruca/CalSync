Full detail copies the title, location, meeting link and guest list. Fixed label uses the word in the next column and reveals nothing else.

Visible to others decides what everyone else sees in the target calendar. Off, they see a titleless "Busy" block even when you see full detail. On, they see it like any other event of yours in that account — useful when the other calendar belongs to a client or project of the same organization.
