Full detail copies the title, location, meeting link and guest list. Fixed label uses the word in the next column and reveals nothing else.

Visible to others decides what everyone else sees in the target calendar. Off, they see a titleless "Busy" block even when you see full detail. On, they see it like any other event of yours in that account — useful when the other calendar belongs to a client or project of the same organisation.

Danger zone

Delete everything CalSync created

Deletes from every account the events CalSync created — busy blocks and protected blocks, on any date — leaving your calendars exactly as they were. Your real events are untouched: the engine can only delete what carries its own marker.

When it finishes it is left in dry run, so nothing is recreated until you say so. If something could not be deleted the log will say which: run it again, and nothing gets duplicated.

If the dashboard is unresponsive there is an equivalent cleanup outside the app: double click cleanup.command with CalSync closed.
